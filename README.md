# NYC-Uber-Data
This repository will analyze data on millions of Uber trips in New York City during the time periods of April to September 2014 and January to June 2015. 
This data was obtained by FiveThirtyEight from the NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission (TLC) by submitting a Freedom of Information Law request on July 20, 2015. 
This data will be analyzed to determine a multitude of things: Volume of trips by date and time of day, Calculation of fares by time and distance, routine of reoccuring passengers, and more.
